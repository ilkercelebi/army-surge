Army Surge

Lead your army, pass the right gates and break the enemy base. Army Surge is a mobile game for iPhone and Android.

Contact & support

Questions, bug reports or purchase issues: clfsoeale@gmail.com. We reply within a few days.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by the App Store or Google Play. To restore purchases on a new device, open the in-game Shop and tap Restore Purchases. To cancel the VIP subscription, use your App Store or Google Play subscription settings.

Your data

Game progress is stored only on your device. You can delete it any time in Settings > Delete My Data. Read the full Privacy Policy.
